## Why
The help dialog reads as a tall wall of prose, wraps its heading awkwardly, and does not have a dedicated small-window layout. Its key events can also reach the practice coordinator.
## What Changes
- Replace the long help sheet with a compact guide: shortcut rows, short practice instructions and expandable explanations.
- Keep the title/close control and footer visible while the body scrolls within the viewport, in both themes.
- Pause active practice or hold a saved-summary countdown when opening help, and isolate dialog keys from practice controls.
## Impact
Frontend presentation and help interaction only. Existing pause/evidence rules remain unchanged. No audio, scoring or storage schema changes; physical verification stays open.
