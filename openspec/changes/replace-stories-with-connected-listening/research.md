# Research and audit basis

Reviewed 2026-11-15 by three research agents and the orchestrator.

- [CWops January 2025 newsletter, printed page 31](https://cwops.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Solid-Copy_2025_January_FINAL.pdf)
  identifies the excessive jump from a few words to whole stories and introduces
  shorter phrase/QSO material. This is practitioner experience, not a controlled trial.
- [CW Academy Intermediate curriculum v2.2, September 18, 2025](https://cwa.cwops.org/wp-content/uploads/Practice-Instructions-Intermediate-ver.2.2.htm)
  uses staged short words, phrases and operating material; longer stories remain
  harder optional listening. Dojo does not reproduce its restricted curriculum texts.
- [Guediche and Fiez, 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8642092/)
  distinguishes transcription from sentence recall in 21 experienced Morse operators.
  Memory contributes beyond perceptual fluency. The study is correlational and does
  not establish a beginner training intervention or this website's effectiveness.
- [Schlaffke et al., 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5049624/)
  studies perceptual versus lexical/semantic Morse tasks in 18 novices learning
  twelve letters. Neural processing differences are not evidence of story-course efficacy.
- [Roediger and Karpicke, 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16507066/) and
  [Butler, 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20804289/) concern retrieval of prose
  content. They motivate trying active recall but do not prove transfer to Morse skill.
- [Lively, Logan and Pisoni, 1993](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3509365/)
  concerns speech-category learning and generalization. Dojo should separately
  observe familiar-item retention and unseen matched material rather than assuming
  that randomized content itself improves transfer.
- [ARRL W1AW practice](https://www.arrl.org/code-practice-files/) illustrates a
  rotating finite plain-text corpus. More titles alone do not define a learning goal.

The product conclusion is to retire Stories' standalone/completion framing and
test a bounded connected-listening supplement. Keep copy, message-detail recall
and self-report separate; comprehension also depends on memory/language proficiency.
Validate usability first and use matched unfamiliar material before any efficacy claim.

The prior UI audit rendered 24 empty/sparse/populated light/dark desktop/mobile
fixtures and reproduced two bugs: selected tabs used identical foreground/background
colors in light mode, and a content failure could be overwritten by Synced with false
zero totals. Word labels measured 7–8px; six story cards put the first mobile graph
about 2400px down; a 48-record word page grew to about 9850px. Full-width populated
story graphs grew to about 684px. Selecting a word lacked a selected state and left
its filtered history offscreen. These observations set concrete redesign criteria.
