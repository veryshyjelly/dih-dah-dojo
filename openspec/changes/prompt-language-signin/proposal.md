## Why
Words, Stories and their progress tabs need a clear invitation to sign in where learners configure practice or inspect progress, with an honest explanation of account synchronization versus browser-local guest history.
## What Changes
- Add a shared guest sign-in panel on both setup pages and both language progress tabs.
- Open the existing Google/GitHub sign-in dialog from the panel.
- Hide the panel for signed-in learners and during active language practice.
- Explain that guest activity remains local and is not automatically imported.
## Impact
Presentation only. Preserve guest exploration, existing OAuth callbacks, owner isolation, history and scoring. Physical timing gate remains open.
