## Why
CW Dojo needs a consistent, legible radio-console presentation and inspectable progress without changing training evidence or real-time execution.

## What Changes
- Introduce a dark navy, amber and cyan console shell, accessible monochrome and ASCII modes, and keyboard navigation.
- Add typed presentation models for dashboard, launcher, recognition, copying, feedback, summaries, audio and progress.
- Plot existing history through pure comparable-context queries, with point/character drilldowns and cached preparation.
- Preserve input, audio, scoring, policy, persisted formats and explicit DELETE reset.

## Impact
Presentation and coordinator navigation only. Physical output/input/display timing and learning effectiveness remain unverified; the existing engineering-readiness task 1.2 remains open.
