# Research basis

Reviewed 2026-11-14. These sources inform the product direction; numerical defaults
in design.md are product choices, not experimentally established training doses.

| Source | Observation | Design implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CW Academy: LCWO ICR Guidelines](https://cwops.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/LCWO-ICR-Guidelines.htm) | Uses short-word training, increasing word length, and holding letters before entering a response. | Provide length filters and a distinct word response loop. |
| [Morse Code World: Word List Trainer](https://morsecode.world/international/trainer/words.html) | Supports prepared lists, problem-character filtering, replay, and flashcard/speech reveals. | Offer repertoire filters and optional disclosure with explicit assistance labeling. |
| [Morse Code Ninja: Learn](https://www.morsecode.ninja/learn) | Uses words restricted to introduced characters and describes the extra difficulty of assembling words and sentences. | Keep word selection repertoire-aware and avoid treating character success as sentence comprehension. |
| [Morse Code Ninja: short stories](https://www.morsecode.ninja/) | Offers graded narrative listening materials. | A story library is an established practice format; Dojo can provide shorter, controllable sentence playback. |
| [Guediche and Fiez, 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8642092/) | Found a relationship between Morse short-term memory and comprehension not explained by perceptual fluency alone. Participants were experienced operators, and the authors note limited power for subtle effects. | Support listening for meaning separately from typed transcription; do not claim a causal beginner-learning benefit. |
| [Morse Code World: Wordsworth timing](https://morsecode.world/international/timing/wordsworth.html) | Preserves ordinary timing within words while extending word gaps, unlike Farnsworth spacing. | Consider as a later explicit timing feature, not a silent change to current C/E semantics. |

The user chose story listening rather than a branching adventure. Recommended
delivery order is word training followed by sentence-based stories. No third-party
story text, word-list compilation, or audio is licensed for reuse merely by being
linked here. Use original reviewed material with documented provenance. The six
repository practice passages are CC0; the five check passages stay reserved.

Evaluate usability and sustained use after release without describing completion,
self-reported recognition, or repeated-text accuracy as verified proficiency.
