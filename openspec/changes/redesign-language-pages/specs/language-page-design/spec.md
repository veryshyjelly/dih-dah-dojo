## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: First-class language page hierarchy
Words and Stories SHALL share Practice's open page header, large two-line title with accent, outlined icon vocabulary and separate action/support panels. The entire experience SHALL NOT be enclosed in a toolbox-style panel.
#### Scenario: Open either setup page
- **WHEN** a learner opens Words or the story library
- **THEN** a clear page heading precedes independently grouped workspace panels and a separate progress summary
### Requirement: Focused word launch and configuration
Words SHALL prioritize a launch card with the current requested character/effective pace and Start word round action. Round choices, editable pace and alphabet selection SHALL remain keyboard accessible in distinct supporting sections.
#### Scenario: Configure a word round
- **WHEN** a learner changes pace, length, vocabulary, response or selected letters
- **THEN** the launch summary reflects those settings and existing round generation/empty-selection safeguards are preserved
### Requirement: Curated story shelf
Stories SHALL use a featured opening story and a balanced responsive shelf with readable titles, non-disclosing descriptions, requested-duration metadata and visible actions. Character guides SHALL remain independent optional disclosures.
#### Scenario: Inspect before listening
- **WHEN** a learner opens a character guide or browses the featured story
- **THEN** future transmission text remains hidden and audio begins only after explicit playback
### Requirement: Responsive and compatible presentation
Both themes SHALL fit 320px, tablet and desktop widths, with visible focus and adequate control targets. Active playback SHALL retain a focused panel and preserve existing lifecycle, scoring and persistence behavior.
#### Scenario: Compact presentation
- **WHEN** setup is viewed at 320px
- **THEN** panels stack in reading order without document overflow and all configuration and progress actions remain reachable
