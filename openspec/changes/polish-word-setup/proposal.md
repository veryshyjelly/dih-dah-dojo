## Why
Word training still presents oversized speed inputs, a raw repertoire string, and
ungrouped controls. It needs the same clear hierarchy as the redesigned story library.
## What Changes
- Compact pace controls, selectable alphabetical letter chips, and grouped round settings.
- A short introduction and a separate Start area with session expectations.
- Subdued data/history disclosures below the primary action.
## Impact
Presentation and repertoire selection UI only. Existing filters, timing, scoring,
persistence, and guided policy are retained. The physical timing gate remains open.
- Align Words and Stories with existing circular header badges and outlined icons
  in setup sections, story cards and primary controls.
