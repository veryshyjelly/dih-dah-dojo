## Why
Dojo teaches individual characters and callsigns but does not yet teach joined
procedural signals. Spelling AR as two letters produces a different sound from
the end-of-message signal. Learners also need to connect a signal to its operating
meaning and use, without mistaking abbreviations or punctuation aliases for new sounds.

## What Changes
- Add explicit atomic prosign scheduling in Rust/WASM and opt-in audio preparation.
- Add a public reference with joined/spaced comparison, sources, and usage caveats.
- Add account-backed recognition, meaning recall, and operating-context rounds.
- Track comparable independent first attempts, repairs, assistance and interruptions
  in dedicated progress, included in existing sync, backup/import, reset and export.
- Preserve native/guided character training, old binary formats and automatic targets.

## Impact
Rust core timing, WASM audio and activity codecs, React navigation/practice/progress,
and the existing opaque activity API. No new dependencies. Original instructional
examples; primary operating references are cited. Physical timing and learning
effectiveness remain unverified; the physical engineering gate stays open.
