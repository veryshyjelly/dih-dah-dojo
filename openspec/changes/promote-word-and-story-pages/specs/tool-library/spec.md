## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Predictable initial tool selection
Tools SHALL select its first remaining option, Your own words, on initial entry.
Word training and Story listening SHALL be available through their main
navigation destinations rather than the Tools menu. Remaining tools SHALL keep
their existing relative order and functionality.

#### Scenario: Open Tools
- **WHEN** a visitor opens Tools
- **THEN** Your own words is selected and its configuration is visible
- **AND** Word training and Story listening are absent from the Tools menu
